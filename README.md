## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 3D Grand Piano in the Background (Three.js WebGL)
- Procedural, high-fidelity 3D Concert Grand Piano:
  - Curved wing rim with bevels and PBR clearcoat lacquer
  - Golden cast-iron harp with acoustic soundholes and webbing beams
  - Natural spruce wood soundboard
  - 28 parallel steel/bronze strings
  - Raised wing lid with brass prop stick
  - Golden engraved nameplate: `★ ROSHAN ARUL • SRM TRICHY ★`
  - Music stand with score
  - 3 fluted legs with brass caster wheels & 3-pedal lyre
  - Contact shadows and subtle floor reflections

### 2. Smooth Scroll-Driven Camera Choreography
- **Hero:** Dramatic 3/4 concert perspective with ambient floating and mouse parallax tilt.
- **About Me:** Camera glides closer to the soundboard, iron harp, and golden strings.
- **Skills & IoT:** Side-profile view highlighting mechanical and structural precision.
- **Projects:** Sweeping overhead cinematic angle over the black lacquer curves.
- **Interactive Finale (Bottom of Page):**
  - **The 3D Grand Piano smoothly aligns to the bottom of the viewport.**
  - **Key indicator badges (computer keyboard keys + musical note names) appear on the piano keys ONLY when docked at the bottom!**

### 3. Interactive Playable Keyboard & Web Audio API Engine
- **Mouse & Touch Playability:** Click or drag (glissando) across 3D white and black keys. Keys visibly tilt down with realistic mechanical spring-back.
- **Computer Keyboard Playability:**
  - **White Keys:** `[A]` C4, `[S]` D4, `[D]` E4, `[F]` F4, `[G]` G4, `[H]` A4, `[J]` B4, `[K]` C5, `[L]` D5, `[;]` E5, `[']` F5
  - **Black Keys:** `[W]` C#4, `[E]` D#4, `[T]` F#4, `[Y]` G#4, `[U]` A#4, `[O]` C#5, `[P]` D#5
- **Acoustic Physical Modeling Synthesis:** Multi-harmonic additive synthesis with inharmonic string dispersion and hammer strike transients. Zero external lag, 100% offline.
- **Sustain Pedal:** Hold `[SPACEBAR]` or click the Sustain button.
- **Octave Picker:** Shift octaves down or up (-8va / Normal / +8va).
- **Auto-Play Demo Melodies:**
  - ▶ *Für Elise* (Ludwig van Beethoven)
  - ▶ *Interstellar Theme* (Hans Zimmer)
  - ▶ *Canon in D* (Johann Pachelbel)

## 💻 How to Run Locally

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari).
2. No build step, node modules, or internet connection required. The Three.js library (`three.min.js`) is packaged directly alongside the project files.
